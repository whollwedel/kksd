What is Kali Kempo?

Kali Kempo Self Defense combines Shaolin Kempo, Kung Fu, and Pekiti Tirsia Kali into a well-rounded martial arts training program focused on practical self-defense, fitness, and personal development.

  • Beginner-friendly classes for kids, teens, and adults
  • Traditional Chinese and Filipino martial arts
  • Practical self-defense, situational awareness, and de-escalation
  • Controlled drills and progressive skills training

Whether you're completely new to martial arts or have previous training, Kali Kempo offers opportunities to develop your skills at your own pace.

Who are classes for?

We currently offer two classes on Monday & Friday evenings:

  • Family Class 6:30–7:30 PM
  • Teens & Adults Class (14+): 7:30–8:30 PM

Our classes welcome everyone, regardless of experience or fitness level. Complete beginners are encouraged to join, and experienced practitioners will find plenty to challenge them too.
Just bring workout clothes, water, and come prepared to train!
