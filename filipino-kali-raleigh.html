What Is Filipino Kali?

Filipino Kali is part of a family of Filipino martial arts often discussed alongside the names Eskrima and Arnis. Training methods vary by system and school, and can include footwork, coordination, partner drills, and the study of weapons-based movement.

At Kali Kempo, the Filipino martial arts component draws from Pekiti Tirsia Kali. It is taught as part of a broader curriculum rather than as a separate, standalone Kali-only class. Check the current curriculum and ask the instructor if you would like to know which topics are being covered.

How Kali Fits Into Kali Kempo

Pekiti Tirsia Kali brings to the table a unique form of footwork, along with a technical philosophy focused around speed and precision. This footwork gets highlighted when used within other arts, as it provides a novel way of moving and applying the strikes learned through those arts. Speed and precision is not only a focus of Pekiti Tirsia Kali, but of both Kempo and Kung Fu, allowing all three to blend well together. Most of the weapon work learned in Kali Kempo Self defense is learned through the lens of Pekiti Tirsia Kali. The primary weapons used are knives, sticks (which also function as a training stand-in for swords and other bladed weapons), and staffs.
