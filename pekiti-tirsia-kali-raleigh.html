Filipino Kali Training in Raleigh, NC

Interested in Filipino martial arts? Kali Kempo Self Defense incorporates Pekiti Tirsia Kali alongside Shaolin Kempo and Kung Fu, bringing Filipino and Chinese martial arts together in one training program.

What Is Pekiti Tirsia Kali?

Pekiti Tirsia Kali is part of a family of Filipino martial arts often discussed alongside the names Eskrima and Arnis. It is a combat-oriented style that focuses on edged, impact, and improvised weapons. Not only how to use them, but also how to defend against them, with techniques tested against experts in their respective weapons. It is heavily footwork and self-defense focused.

How Kali Fits Into Kali Kempo

Pekiti Tirsia Kali brings to the table a unique form of footwork, along with a technical philosophy focused around speed and precision. This footwork gets highlighted when used within other arts, as it provides a novel way of moving and applying the strikes learned through those arts. Speed and precision is not only a focus of Pekiti Tirsia Kali, but of both Kempo and Kung Fu, allowing all three to blend well together.

Most of the weapon work learned in Kali Kempo Self defense is learned through the lens of Pekiti Tirsia Kali. The primary weapons used are knives, sticks (which also function as a training stand-in for swords and other bladed weapons), and staffs.
